# Third-party assets

The models and textures in this folder were converted and restyled for Wirelight from the packs and
scans listed in [`CREDITS.md`](../../../../CREDITS.md). They are here as part of the game, not as an
asset pack. Wirelight's MIT licence does not cover them: each keeps its own licence (CC0, CC BY 4.0
or the Quaternius Asset License), and the CC BY ones need their credit kept.

If you want these assets for your own project, download them from their authors (links in
`CREDITS.md`) and follow each one's licence. Please don't repackage this folder as a pack.
